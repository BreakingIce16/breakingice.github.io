Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

check-in is a personal app used by one person, its owner. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, check-in uses Google APIs to read his calendars, create and change calendar events, and read and update his Google Tasks lists.

How the data is used

This data is used only to help the owner manage his own schedule and lists. It is processed by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude API) to generate replies, and is otherwise kept on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

check-in's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
